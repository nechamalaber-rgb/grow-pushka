Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your My Grow Pushka account and all associated data directly from within the app.

Steps to delete your account:
  1. Open the My Grow Pushka app at buildbitachon.org
  2. Tap the Settings icon (⚙️) in the bottom navigation
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, name, password)
  • Your pushka balance and coin history
  • Your donation history
  • Your reminder preferences
  • All personal data stored in our database

What is retained

Stripe may retain payment records for legal and compliance purposes. We do not control Stripe's data retention policies.

Need help?

If you're unable to access the app or need assistance, email us at adlaber@gmail.com and we will delete your account manually within 7 days.
